# Legible Studio — Business Overview

## Company Summary

**Company Name:** Legible Studio (or Legible Studios)

**Tagline:** "Design decisions you can read."

**Mission:** Replace opaque CAD workflows with transparent, traceable design systems. Every line in a drawing traces back to a decision. Every decision traces back to a question.

**Category:** Construction technology / Design software / AEC (Architecture, Engineering, Construction)

---

## The Problem

### For Designers & Architects

- CAD software is a blank canvas — you start from zero every project
- Design decisions are buried in geometry, not documented
- Iteration is slow: draw, review, redraw, review, redraw
- Knowledge lives in people's heads, not in the system
- Software built for drafting, not for design thinking

### For Builders & Contractors

- Plans arrive as static documents — no intelligence, just lines
- Layout requires expensive surveying equipment and skilled operators
- Errors discovered in the field are expensive to fix
- No connection between design intent and construction execution
- Coordination failures between office and site

### For Project Owners

- Can't trace why decisions were made
- Change orders lack context — was this always the intent?
- No audit trail for regulatory compliance
- Design fees don't correlate to value delivered

---

## The Solution

### Legible Studio

One application that grows with your design. Three modes that emerge as work progresses:

| Mode | What It Does | Value |
|------|--------------|-------|
| **LegiQBD** | Capture design intent through questions and constraints. Solver generates optimal layouts. | Skip weeks of iteration. Get to a viable design in hours. |
| **LegiCAD** | Refine geometry with full rendering built in. Every edit is tracked. | Design and visualize in one place. Complete audit trail. |
| **LegiDoc** | Generate sheets, schedules, permit packages. Signoff workflows. | Documentation writes itself. Compliance is automatic. |

**Key differentiator:** The tools don't exist until you've done the thinking. You can't skip to CAD because there's nothing to draw until you've answered the questions. This enforces quality and creates the audit trail automatically.

### Field Products

| Product | What It Is | Value |
|---------|------------|-------|
| **LegiSite** | Motorized tracking station + phone app | Replace $40,000+ robotic total stations with a $X,XXX system. No surveying expertise required. |
| **LegiView** | Tethered XR safety glasses | See the design overlaid on the real world. Verify before you build. |
| **LegiLens** | XR software layer | Hardware-agnostic AR. Works on LegiView, third-party glasses, tablets, phones. |

## How It Works

### Design Phase (Office)

```
Client describes what they need (conversation)
         ↓
LegiQBD captures requirements as structured data
         ↓
Solver generates optimal floor plan
         ↓
Designer refines in LegiCAD
         ↓
LegiDoc produces permit-ready drawings
         ↓
Complete audit trail: every line → decision → question
```

### Construction Phase (Field)

```
LegiDoc exports coordinates
         ↓
LegiSite app receives layout points
         ↓
LegiSite station projects points on site
         ↓
Crew builds to projected locations
         ↓
LegiLens verifies against design
         ↓
As-built data flows back to LegiDoc
```

**The audit trail is continuous.** A nail in a wall traces back through the station, through the app, through the schema, to the original client conversation.

## Why Now?

### Technology Tailwinds

1. **LLMs are production-ready.** Natural language as interface is now viable.
2. **GPU compute is cheap.** Real-time rendering and simulation on commodity hardware.
3. **XR is maturing.** AR glasses are approaching usability for field work.
4. **Construction labor shortage.** Industry must adopt technology to do more with fewer people.

### Market Tailwinds

1. **Regulatory pressure.** Building codes increasingly require documentation and traceability.
2. **Insurance pressure.** Liability concerns drive demand for audit trails.
3. **Sustainability requirements.** Energy modeling and compliance need integrated tools.
4. **Generational shift.** Younger workers expect modern software, not 1990s interfaces.

### Competitive Window

- Autodesk is slow-moving and acquisition-focused
- Startups are adding AI as a feature, not rethinking workflow
- Hardware incumbents aren't building software platforms
- No one is connecting design intent to field execution with full traceability

**The window is open.** First mover with an integrated platform wins.
